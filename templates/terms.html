Last updated: October 2026

1. What Spare does

Spare is a platform where individuals resell event tickets to each other. Spare itself does not sell tickets or organise events.

2. Selling

Only sell tickets that are yours, that are valid, and that you are not offering anywhere else. Your price can be at most {{ 'the face value' if price_cap == 100 else price_cap ~ '% of the face value' }}. You are responsible for following the organiser's terms.

3. Buying and fees

Buyers pay the seller's price plus a service fee of {{ fee_label }} per ticket. Sellers pay no fees.

4. Payment and payouts

Payments are processed by Stripe. The amount is held and paid out to the seller {{ payout_hours }} hours after the night of the event, unless the buyer reports a problem before then.

5. Problems and refunds

If a ticket doesn't work, report it from your order before the payout. After review we refund the full amount. Sellers who list invalid or double-sold tickets are blocked and may be held liable.
