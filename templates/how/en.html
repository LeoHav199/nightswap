{{ t('faq') }}

Am I allowed to resell my ticket?

Usually yes, but some organisers ban resale or print your name on the ticket. Check the event's terms before you sell.

Which tickets can I list?

Two kinds. A ticket file: a PDF or image with a QR code or barcode, which the buyer downloads. Or a ticket that lives in an app ({{ apps }} or another app with a transfer button): the buyer gives the name and email of their account in that app, you send it with the app's own transfer button and tap "{{ t('seller_transfer_done_btn') }}", and the buyer confirms it arrived.

What if the seller sells the same ticket somewhere else too?

Report it from your order before the seller is paid. You get your money back and the seller is blocked.

When do I get paid as a seller?

If the buyer confirms on the day of the event that the ticket worked, straight away. Otherwise {{ payout_hours }} hours after the night of the event, as long as the buyer hasn't reported a problem. For a ticket in an app, only once you've marked it as transferred. You set up payouts once through Stripe with your IBAN.

Can I edit or remove my listing?

You can change the price on your listing's page, as long as it stays within the maximum. You can remove it any time before it sells.
