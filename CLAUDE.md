# Spare (formerly Nightswap)

Ticket resale for students in Rotterdam, at sparetickets.eu: club nights, student parties, and some festivals and concerts. Owner: Leo. The site is in English and Dutch, plus French, German and Spanish.

## Product decisions (already made, don't reopen without asking)
- Launch city is **Rotterdam only** (`ENABLED_CITIES`, `SHOW_ALL_NETHERLANDS=0`). The code supports more cities for later.
- Prices are capped at **double the face value** since 5 Nov 2026 (`PRICE_CAP_PERCENT=200`; Leo and Ilyas chose it over no cap, which felt odd to buyers, and over the earlier face value + 20%, which felt too strict for sellers; may change later). The texts say "never more than double" in every language (`*_double` keys, picked by `i18n.t` when pct is 100); other values say "X% above". The face value is shown next to the price with the markup (`markup_percent`). With a cap, listings need the official price set on the event or proof of the face value plus an admin check (#25), so fill in official prices in /admin/events. `NIGHTSWAP_PRICE_CAP_PERCENT=0` means no cap at all (no proof or review; texts switch via `price_free`). Belgium is believed to ban resale above the original price: check the law before expanding. Fees (since 28 Oct 2026, chosen so every sale makes money): buyers pay **8%, minimum €1.99** (`FEE_PERCENT`/`FEE_MIN_CENTS`; the minimum was €1.50 until 6 Nov 2026); sellers pay **5%, minimum €1**, plus a **10% profit share** on whatever their price is above the face value (`PROFIT_SHARE_PERCENT`, Leo and Ilyas's choice on 6 Nov 2026; 0 = off), taken from the payout (`SELLER_FEE_PERCENT`/`SELLER_FEE_MIN_CENTS`, `seller_fee_for(price, face)`, stored per order in `seller_fee_cents`; older orders pay out in full). Keep asking and showing the face value: the profit share and the price cap depend on it. Why: Stripe NL charges €0.29 per iDEAL payment, 1.5% + €0.25 per EEA card, and for Connect €2 per seller per month they're paid plus 0.25% + €0.10 per payout, so the old buyer-only fee lost about €0.84 on a one-off €20 sale. A flat €1 fee was tried earlier and dropped too.
- Contact details are required for safety: an email code at sign-in, a phone code before selling, and sellers must be 18+. Admins open /admin with an authenticator-app code every 12 hours (#23, `totp.py`; recovery codes; `flask --app app reset-admin-2fa <email>`).
- Money safety (#26, #27): payouts and refunds claim the order first (`releasing`/`refunding`) and use Stripe idempotency keys; chargebacks hold the payout or take it back (`take_back_payout`); /admin can refund after payout; first-time buyers pay by iDEAL only.
- **Swap (5 Nov 2026):** Spare holds the buyer's money until they're in. From when the doors open (`SWAP_OPENS_AFTER_DOORS_MINUTES`) the buyer slides "Swap" on the order page (`swap_order`, `swap()`; a plain button without JavaScript); that pays the seller straight away if the seller has 2+ completed sales and the buyer paid by iDEAL (`SWAP_INSTANT_IDEAL_ONLY`, looked up with `payments.payment_method_type`; card payments can be charged back, so those wait). Without a swap, sellers are paid 24h after the night (`PAYOUT_DELAY_HOURS`); new sellers (<2 released sales, #28) 72h, never early. About 45 minutes after the doors the buyer gets "Inside? Slide Swap" (push + email, `send_reminders`). Buyers can report a problem until the money has gone; messages pushing buyers to swap early are flagged `rate_early`. New sellers have 2 tickets on sale at most and their listings wait (`draft`) until Stripe payout setup is done. The seller's Stripe payout account gets a prefilled product description. Buyers get refunds if an event is cancelled or a ticket is disputed.
- Lying buyers (6 Nov 2026, Leo's worry: getting in and then claiming they didn't): door problems (`AUTO_REFUND_KINDS`) need a photo, can only be reported until 06:00 after the doors (`door_report_until`) and never after Swap; the report form warns that reports are checked with the organiser; /admin/disputes has a ready Dutch/English email to the organiser with the ticket's code (`organiser_mail`) and a "False report: pay the seller and block the buyer" decision (`keep_block`). The terms (§8) say so in all 5 languages.
- Most sellers sell once, so the track-record badge only appears after 2+ sales.
- Messages hide phone numbers, handles and links. Attempts to pay or deal outside Spare (other apps incl. typos, Tikkie/cash/crypto, meeting up to pay, "pay you directly", in Dutch too) are **not delivered** since 28 Oct 2026: the sender sees why, the admin page keeps the original (`moderation.py`, flag `blocked`).
- Venue photos come from Wikimedia Commons, credited on `/credits` and not on the images. When no photo exists, a generated poster is shown (`art.py`).
- The homepage shows events with tickets first, then a compact "Coming up" list, so it never looks empty.
- Name: **Spare** (renamed from Nightswap on 27 Oct 2026; Leo and his partner chose it). Domain sparetickets.eu (chosen over sparetickets.nl so it can serve other countries later; spare.nl and sparetickets.com are taken).
- Logo: a tilted lime ticket with a dashed tear line plus a lowercase "spare" wordmark (Inter Tight 900), lime `#d4ff3a` on navy `#15132b`, off-white text `#f5f3ec`. Keep the brand on dark backgrounds; lime is unreadable on white. The PNG icons are drawn by `tools/make_icons.py`.
- Internal names keep "nightswap" on purpose: the repo, the Render service and disk, `NIGHTSWAP_*` settings, the database file and the preview server names. Renaming those would break the live site or make Render create a new empty service.
- Coming-soon mode (before the real launch): switched on and off at /admin → Launch, stored in the `settings` table, so no redeploy. Visitors see a sign-up page (`coming_soon.html`); admins and friends with the preview link (`/preview/<code>`, renewable) see the full site. Sign-ups go into the `waitlist` table; /admin/waitlist lists them, exports CSV and sends the launch email in batches of 20, each with a remove-me link.
- Marketing links: /admin/sources makes a `?from=<code>` link with a print-ready QR (SVG) per flyer batch, post, association or ambassador. Visits are counted once per session in `source_visits`; the first link a visitor came from is saved on their waitlist entry and account (`source`).
- Personal touches: cartoon-character profile pictures (`avatars.py` draws them from parts; no photo uploads, nothing to moderate) that are earned by selling (`avatars.UNLOCK_AT`: 1, 3, 5 and 10 paid sales, refunds don't count), picked at /settings and shown next to names, so a character means "has sold here before"; icons from the very first version map to a character. Opt-in tap sounds for the bottom menu made with Web Audio (off by default, saved per device in localStorage, plus optional vibration on Android), and a small "Tonight" pop-up after 12:00 at most once a day, never on selling, listing, checkout or chat pages. The server runs on Amsterdam time (`TZ` in render.yaml).
- "People looking": the number of people with "Notify me" on for a night (`looking_count`) shows as a banner on the event page (with a Sell button when nobody sells yet), in the sell form's event picker and on cards ("3 looking"), to pull in sellers.
- Day-before resell reminder: from 12:00 (`RESELL_HOUR`) the day before an event, buyers holding a ticket bought on Spare get one email + push "Can't make it? Sell it" (`send_resell_reminders`, part of /tasks/hourly; `orders.resell_reminded_at`). The duplicate-ticket check (`already_listed`) lets only the most recent buyer of a ticket list it again, and never while it's on sale.
- Safety nets: a nightly backup (`backup.py`, run by /tasks/hourly around 03:00) emails the admins a zip of the database plus uploads (uploads left out above 15 MB) and keeps 3 database-only copies in DATA_DIR/backups. Backups are encrypted with `BACKUP_KEY` (Fernet; `flask --app app backup-key` makes one, `decrypt-backup <file>` opens one); on the real site nothing is sent or stored without it. Ticket files are deleted 14 days after the event once the order is paid out or refunded (`cleanup_ticket_files`, hashes kept for the duplicate check); admins get an email when the disk passes 70% and /admin shows disk usage; /admin has "Download backup". Crashes email the admins with the traceback (at most 5 an hour). The "phone verified" badge only shows when real SMS is on (`SMS_ENABLED`). Fonts are self-hosted in static/fonts (no Google requests, for privacy).
- Two kinds of tickets (since 28 Oct 2026): a **file** (PDF/screenshot, duplicate-QR check) or **in an app** with a changing QR code (`listings.delivery='transfer'`, app in `config.TRANSFER_APPS`). App tickets: the buyer gives the name and email of their ticket-app account at purchase (shown only to the seller), the seller transfers with the app's own button and taps "I've transferred it", the buyer taps "I've got it". No payout until it's marked transferred (or the buyer confirms); sellers get one reminder after `TRANSFER_REMIND_HOURS`.
- Code in static/js/app.js that should run on every page must go above the sell-form section: that part ends with `if (!sell) return;`.
- Visitor IPs (rate limits): Render sits behind Cloudflare, so `TRUST_PROXY_HOPS=2` (checked 4 Nov 2026 on /admin → Network check; with 1, everyone looked like a Cloudflare address).
- Look and feel: professional nightlife style that shouldn't look AI-generated. Day tabs, poster cards, and a bottom tab bar on phones.
- Phones first (5 Nov 2026, from a phone audit): on the event night (noon to noon, `door_night`) the buyer's order page opens in door mode, with the code, the downloads and Swap first and the photo and details below. The event page puts the tickets straight after the name, with a short photo strip on phones. Photos are served as WebP copies at 480/800/1280px (`/posters/<file>?w=`, `poster_copy`, kept in DATA_DIR/cache, outside the backups); og:image keeps the original. Still to do from that audit: the sell form ("You receive" above the Post button, price proof only when needed, an easier date picker, the phone check at the end), moving the Report link away from the price, the Account buttons on small phones, a "check your junk folder" hint on the code screen, a lighter footer on app pages, and the "All · 1" day tab.

## Stack
- Flask 3, SQLite in WAL mode, Jinja templates, plain JS and CSS, no build step. Python 3.9 locally, 3.11 on Render.
- `app.py` holds all the routes. The rest: `db.py` (schema plus migrations in `ADDED_COLUMNS`), `config.py` (all settings come from env vars or `.env`), `payments.py` (Stripe Checkout and Connect Accounts **v2**), `notify.py` (SMTP email, Twilio SMS), `push.py` (web push), `wallet.py` (Apple Wallet), `ticketcodes.py` (duplicate QR check), `importer.py` (schema.org event import), `i18n.py` + `locales/` (translations).
- Installable web app: `static/js/sw.js` (bump `VERSION` when caching changes); the buyer's paid order pages are kept offline in the `spare-orders` cache when the server sends `X-Spare-Offline`, forgotten 3 days after the event and on sign-out, the manifest is served by `app.py`, and static links carry `?v=hash`.
- Without a Stripe key it uses a pretend checkout. Without SMTP or Twilio, sign-in codes are printed and shown on screen (development only).

## Ideas already weighed
- Spare buying early-bird tickets itself and reselling them: **rejected**. It breaks the face-value promise, organisers can void bulk-resold tickets, it competes with our own sellers, and we'd carry the inventory risk and legal grey zone.
- First-hand tickets for organisers with Spare-issued rotating QR codes and a door scanner (the only full fix for double selling, review #33): planned in `docs/organiser-ticketing.md`, not built.
- To fix the shortage of sellers instead (suggested, not built yet): first-hand ticket sales for student associations and small organisers (strongest), commission through official ticket-shop referral links, showing "X people are looking" to bring out sellers, reminders before the night, and official organiser allocations later.
