## Product decisions (already made, don't reopen without asking)
- Launch city is **Rotterdam only** (`ENABLED_CITIES`, `SHOW_ALL_NETHERLANDS=0`). The code supports more cities for later.
- Prices are capped at **double the face value** since 5 Nov 2026 (`PRICE_CAP_PERCENT=200`; Leo and Ilyas chose it over no cap, which felt odd to buyers, and over the earlier face value + 20%, which felt too strict for sellers; may change later). The texts say "never more than double" in every language (`*_double` keys, picked by `i18n.t` when pct is 100); other values say "X% above". The face value is shown next to the price with the markup (`markup_percent`). With a cap, a listing can't go above the event's official price when one is set. The price proof upload for events without an official price (#25, `FACE_PROOF_REQUIRED`) is **off since 14 Nov 2026** (Leo: too much friction for sellers); the face value typed in is trusted, the terms still say we may ask for proof, and `NIGHTSWAP_FACE_PROOF=1` switches the upload and admin check back on. Filling in official prices in /admin/events still keeps prices honest. `NIGHTSWAP_PRICE_CAP_PERCENT=0` means no cap at all (no proof or review; texts switch via `price_free`). Belgium is believed to ban resale above the original price: check the law before expanding. Fees (since 28 Oct 2026, chosen so every sale makes money): buyers pay **8%, minimum €1.99** (`FEE_PERCENT`/`FEE_MIN_CENTS`; the minimum was €1.50 until 6 Nov 2026); sellers pay **5%, minimum €0.99** (€1 until 14 Nov 2026, when Leo and Ilyas also had the sell summary reworked to read price, fee, you receive, with the buyer total and its fee below), plus a **10% profit share** on whatever their price is above the face value (`PROFIT_SHARE_PERCENT`, Leo and Ilyas's choice on 6 Nov 2026; 0 = off), taken from the payout (`SELLER_FEE_PERCENT`/`SELLER_FEE_MIN_CENTS`, `seller_fee_for(price, face)`, stored per order in `seller_fee_cents`; older orders pay out in full). Keep asking and showing the face value: the profit share and the price cap depend on it. Why: Stripe NL charges €0.29 per iDEAL payment, 1.5% + €0.25 per EEA card, and for Connect €2 per seller per month they're paid plus 0.25% + €0.10 per payout, so the old buyer-only fee lost about €0.84 on a one-off €20 sale. A flat €1 fee was tried earlier and dropped too.
- Official prices fill themselves in (9 Nov 2026, Leo didn't want to type them): the hourly task (`fill_official_prices`, last, after the heartbeat, 12-second budget) reads the ticket page of each event in the next 30 days without a price and takes the schema.org price of the highest ticket type, the same rule as Import from link; only a match on date and name counts, a price set in /admin is never overwritten, each event is tried once a day (`events.price_checked_at`). Stager pages (Rotown, V11, Maassilo shows) work; Annabel, BIRD, Toffler and ticketshop.eu pages have no such data, so those events still use the seller's proof plus an admin check unless a price is typed in /admin/events. `NIGHTSWAP_PRICE_LOOKUP=0` switches it off (tests do). Tests: tests/official_prices_test.py.
- Contact details are required for safety: an email code at sign-in, a phone code before selling, and sellers must be 18+. Admins open /admin with an authenticator-app code every 12 hours (#23, `totp.py`; recovery codes; `flask --app app reset-admin-2fa <email>`).
- Repeated Stripe dispute deliveries are ignored (7 Nov 2026, `handle_stripe_money_event`: no second email, no second reversal). Pause sales is described further down.
- Money safety (#26, #27): payouts and refunds claim the order first (`releasing`/`refunding`) and use Stripe idempotency keys; chargebacks hold the payout or take it back (`take_back_payout`); /admin can refund after payout; first-time buyers pay by iDEAL only.
- **Swap (5 Nov 2026):** Spare holds the buyer's money until they're in. From when the doors open (`SWAP_OPENS_AFTER_DOORS_MINUTES`) the buyer slides "Swap" on the order page (`swap_order`, `swap()`; a plain button without JavaScript); that pays the seller straight away if the seller has 2+ completed sales and the buyer paid by iDEAL (`SWAP_INSTANT_IDEAL_ONLY`, looked up with `payments.payment_method_type`; card payments can be charged back, so those wait). Without a swap, sellers are paid 24h after the night (`PAYOUT_DELAY_HOURS`); new sellers (<2 released sales, #28) also 24h (`NEW_SELLER_PAYOUT_HOURS`, 72h until 6 Nov 2026), but never early at Swap. About 45 minutes after the doors the buyer gets "Inside? Slide Swap" (push + email, `send_reminders`). Buyers can report a problem until the money has gone; messages pushing buyers to swap early are flagged `rate_early`. New sellers have 2 tickets on sale at most and their listings wait (`draft`) until Stripe payout setup is done. The seller's Stripe payout account gets a prefilled product description. Buyers get refunds if an event is cancelled or a ticket is disputed.
- Lying buyers (6 Nov 2026, Leo's worry: getting in and then claiming they didn't): door problems (`AUTO_REFUND_KINDS`) need a photo, can only be reported until 06:00 after the doors (`door_report_until`) and never after Swap; the report form warns that reports are checked with the organiser; /admin/disputes has a ready Dutch/English email to the organiser with the ticket's code (`organiser_mail`) and a "False report: pay the seller and block the buyer" decision (`keep_block`). ## Stack
- Flask 3, SQLite in WAL mode, Jinja templates, plain JS and CSS, no build step. Python 3.9 locally, 3.11 on Render.
- `app.py` holds all the routes. The rest: `db.py` (schema plus migrations in `ADDED_COLUMNS`), `config.py` (all settings come from env vars or `.env`), `payments.py` (Stripe Checkout and Connect Accounts **v2**), `notify.py` (SMTP email, Twilio SMS), `push.py` (web push), `wallet.py` (Apple Wallet), `ticketcodes.py` (duplicate QR check), `importer.py` (schema.org event import), `i18n.py` + `locales/` (translations).
- Installable web app: `static/js/sw.js` (bump `VERSION` when caching changes); the buyer's paid order pages are kept offline in the `spare-orders` cache when the server sends `X-Spare-Offline`, forgotten 3 days after the event and on sign-out, the manifest is served by `app.py`, and static links carry `?v=hash`.
- Without a Stripe key it uses a pretend checkout. Without SMTP or Twilio, sign-in codes are printed and shown on screen (development only).

## Ideas already weighed
- Spare buying early-bird tickets itself and reselling them: **rejected**. It breaks the face-value promise, organisers can void bulk-resold tickets, it competes with our own sellers, and we'd carry the inventory risk and legal grey zone.
- First-hand tickets for organisers with Spare-issued rotating QR codes and a door scanner (the only full fix for double selling, review #33): planned in `docs/organiser-ticketing.md`, not built.
- To fix the shortage of sellers instead (suggested, not built yet): first-hand ticket sales for student associations and small organisers (strongest), commission through official ticket-shop referral links, showing "X people are looking" to bring out sellers, reminders before the night, and official organiser allocations later.
